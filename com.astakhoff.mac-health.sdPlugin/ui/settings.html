CPU WARNING THRESHOLDS

Percent of total CPU capacity, averaged over up to 10 seconds.

DISK WARNING THRESHOLDS

Warn when either available percentage or available GiB reaches its threshold. This is capacity, not disk activity or drive health.

STATUS COLORS

Green: normal. Yellow: warning. Red: critical. Colors are fixed; status words appear here in settings, not on the key. Gray means readings are unavailable.

Updates every 2 seconds while a Mac Health key is visible. Each metric is a separate action. Press the key to open Activity Monitor, or Disk Utility for Disk.
